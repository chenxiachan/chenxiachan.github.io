Open-source tools that put my research into practice: AI that keeps people in charge of their own understanding.

## ThoughtDAG

**AI conversations that branch on an infinite canvas.** Each exchange becomes a node, and the wires are the context: explore a side question, connect useful paths, and choose what the model sees next.

Extended with researchers at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in TraceVista.

[Website](https://chenxiachan.github.io/thoughtdag/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/chenxiachan/thoughtdag)

## PaperFold

**Zoom any paper for your better understanding.** An arXiv paper unfolds in five levels, from a one-screen map of its sections to every word, in your language. AI sets how much you see; the understanding stays yours.

Developed with the Munich Data Science Institute.

[Try it in your browser](https://chenxiachan.github.io/paperfold-gallery/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/chenxiachan/paperfold)

## Compositional Knowledge Primitives (CKP)

**A research programme for the loop between knowledge embedding and discovery.** A shared method layer between researcher and agent, so that agents can widen exploration while people keep the judgment. Presented at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development in 2026; a DFG proposal is under review.
