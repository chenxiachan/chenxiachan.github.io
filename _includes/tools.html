TextFold ↗CInteraction pattern alignment

AI that raises our cognitive efficiency: text folds into five levels of detail, from a few words down to the author’s own sentences, so you choose how much to read.

  • A wider cognitive bandwidth, as AI writes more than people can read
  • A new interaction pattern: every level opens back to the source
  • Aligned with how people read

Reads web pages, arXiv, PubMed Central, bioRxiv, medRxiv, Wikipedia and GitHub; PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, EPUB, HTML, CSV, images and Markdown; and an agent’s replies.
Works with your browser, Claude Code, Pi, Zotero and Obsidian; desktop app for macOS and Windows.
